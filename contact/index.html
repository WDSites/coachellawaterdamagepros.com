Before You Call

What to Have Ready

  • Your address and ZIP code, so we can tell you whether a crew can reach you.
  • What is wet and where the water came from, even if you are not sure of the cause.
  • Whether the water has reached outlets, appliances or the electrical panel.
  • Whether the water is clean, from a sewer or from a storm drain.
  • Your insurer's name if you plan to file a claim, and a few dated photos of the damage.

If you see sparks, smell gas or water has reached the panel, leave the area and call 911 or the utility first. Then call us.

After You Call

What Happens Next

You will hear safety steps first, such as which valve to close and what to lift off the floor. Then a crew is arranged, and the scope of work and how the price is set are explained before anyone starts. Same-day response is available when crews can reach you, and we do not promise an arrival time.

For the services themselves, see the services index. To check whether your town is covered, see the service area page or just ask on the call.
