Where Leaks Start in a Subdivision Home

  • A garage water heater, where a slow tank leak can run under a wall into the house
  • A washer or dishwasher hose, which lets go behind the machine where no one sees it
  • A supply line under a bathroom or kitchen sink, usually at a fitting or a braided connector
  • An air conditioner condensate line that backs up and soaks a ceiling or closet floor
  • A roof or flashing leak that shows up as a stain after heavy rain

Most single-family homes here sit on concrete slabs, as is common in desert construction. A leak that reaches the slab spreads across the floor and into the bottom of the walls, so flooring and baseboards are the first places to check.

What to Tell the Dispatcher

Give the street, which side of Van Buren Street you are on and whether the water is clean, from a supply line, or contaminated, from a drain or toilet. Mention the room, how long it has been running and whether the water is shut off.

Sewer Backups and Clean Water Are Different Jobs

The City of Coachella Utilities Department provides water and wastewater service to city customers. If sewage is backing up from the street line, the department lists a separate sewer-overflow standby number on its website, and its office is closed on Fridays.

Sewage is contaminated water. Keep children and pets out, do not use a household vacuum on it and do not try to dry it yourself. Contaminated materials usually have to be removed and cleaned, not just dried.

A First-Hour Checklist

  1. Close the main shutoff, or the appliance valve, and open a low faucet to drain pressure.
  2. Stay out of standing water if any outlet, cord or appliance is nearby, and leave breakers alone while you are wet.
  3. Photograph the source and each wet surface before moving anything.
  4. Lift rugs, boxes and electronics off wet floors.
  5. Call a plumber for the repair and a restoration company for the water and drying.

Insurance and Flood Wording

Sudden leaks are often handled under a homeowners policy, and slow leaks often are not, so read your policy or ask your insurer before you decide. We record readings and photos for your file, and the insurer decides coverage. Flood and mudslide are typically excluded from standard policies, according to the California Department of Insurance.

Questions From Rancho Las Flores Homeowners

Where is Rancho Las Flores?

A 2007 City of Coachella report places the subdivision east of Van Buren Street between Avenues 48 and 49. Rancho Las Flores Park is on Van Buren Street. Give your cross streets when you call.

My garage water heater is leaking into the house. What do I do first?

Close the cold water supply valve to the tank and shut off its power or gas control if it is safe to reach. Then call. Water that has crossed into a wall or floor needs moisture mapping and drying.

Can I dry a wet floor with fans myself?

Small spills of clean water can be dried quickly. Water that has soaked carpet pad, baseboards or drywall needs measured drying, and sewage or drain water should never be handled as a do-it-yourself job.

Does insurance cover a leak from a washer hose?

Often a sudden failure is covered and a slow, ignored leak is not, but it depends on the policy. Ask your insurer. We document the loss and make no promise about coverage.
