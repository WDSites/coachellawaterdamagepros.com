Core Stages

The Stages of a Water Damage Job

Water damage work is a sequence. Water is stopped, pulled out, dried, cleaned up and then rebuilt, and mold becomes a separate job when moisture lingers. These four pages and the mold page cover the stages in order. The overall process, from the first call to a dry and repaired home, is laid out on the water damage restoration homepage.

Emergency water removal

Pumping and extraction while the water is still there. Open the page.

Water mitigation

Stopping the spread in the first days with drying and moisture mapping. Open the page.

Water damage cleanup

Wet debris, sanitizing and deodorizing after the source is stopped. Open the page.

Water damage repair

Rebuilding drywall, ceilings and floors once the structure is dry. Open the page.

Mold remediation

Containment, removal and clearance testing after moisture feeds mold. Open the page.
