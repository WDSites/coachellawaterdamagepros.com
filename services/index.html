Choose the Service That Matches What You See

Water damage work is a sequence. Water is stopped, pulled out, dried, cleaned up and then rebuilt, and mold becomes a separate job when moisture lingers. Each service page on this site covers one stage and answers one question, so you can land on the page that fits your situation instead of reading all of them.

If you are not sure which stage you are in, call the 24/7 line and describe what you see. The overall process, from the first call to a dry and repaired home, is laid out on the water damage restoration homepage.

How the Pages Differ

One Question per Service Page

Stopping the damage

Emergency water removal and water mitigation are the two urgent stages. Removal is about getting standing water out quickly. Mitigation is about drying what remains and keeping damage from spreading into wall cavities and flooring, which is usually the larger task.

Cleaning up

Cleanup begins after the source is controlled. It deals with wet debris, damaged materials that cannot be dried, and surfaces that need sanitizing. Flood damage restoration is the version of this work for storm water and sewage, where the water itself is contaminated and the safety steps are stricter.

Rebuilding and mold

Repair is the last stage for most jobs: new drywall, insulation, paint, flooring and cabinets. Mold remediation sits apart because it follows moisture that was not dried in time. It works to a published standard, and independent testing can confirm the result.

Callers often ask for a quote first. Price depends on the water category, the area and materials affected, the drying time and the rebuild work, and the scope and price are explained before work starts.
