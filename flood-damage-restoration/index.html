While you wait for the crew, stay out of standing water, close the main water valve if a plumbing line is the source, lift valuables off the floor and keep children and pets out of the room.

Flood damage restoration removes storm water, drain backups and other contaminated water from a building, takes out materials that cannot be saved and dries the structure so rebuilding can begin. It differs from emergency water removal, which pulls standing water out after a plumbing failure. This page covers the larger and dirtier jobs.

When Floodwater in Your Home Is an Emergency

Treat it as an emergency when water is entering from outside, rising from a floor drain, or sitting against drywall, carpet or cabinets. Storms do not keep business hours, so night and weekend calls are routine, and the line is answered at all of them.

Safety first, before you call

If water has reached outlets, appliances or the electrical panel, do not walk through it. Cut power at the panel only if you can reach it while standing on dry ground. Otherwise leave the house and call 911 or the electric utility before calling us.

Stay away from gas appliances in a flooded area. Storm water and sewage carry contamination, so avoid contact and do not run the washer, dishwasher or toilets until a backed-up drain is cleared.

  • Water is coming in through doors, garage thresholds or low windows
  • A drain or toilet is pushing water or sewage up
  • Standing water has soaked carpet, baseboards or the bottom of walls
  • An overflowing canal, storm drain or irrigation line has reached the property
  • Mud and debris have come in with the water

Why Coachella Floods Fast on Little Rain

The valley floor sits about 69 feet below sea level, and desert ground sheds water instead of absorbing it. A normal year brings only about 5 inches of rain, so one storm can deliver a large share of that in hours. A low corner of a yard or a street that dips toward a drain is where water gathers first.

Hilary in August 2023 closed roads across the Coachella Valley, including I-10, and NASA's Earth Observatory described Palm Springs getting six months of rain in six hours. In October 2026 the remnants of Tropical Storm Rachel dropped 1.71 inches at Thermal, according to National Weather Service totals reported by KESQ. Flat roofs held ponded water, and gutters and drains clogged with palm and tamarisk debris.

To see how your own address is mapped, use the FEMA Flood Map Service Center. Do not assume a flood zone for your street without looking it up, because the answer decides what insurance you can buy.

Basement Flooding Cleanup: Garages, Low Spots and Below-Grade Areas

Most homes in Coachella sit on concrete slabs, as is common in desert construction, so a true basement is the exception. Flooding here usually shows up in garages, sunken patios, entry steps, stairwells that drop below grade and any ground-floor room with a low threshold.

Cleanup follows the same order wherever the water collects. Crews pump or extract the standing water, remove wet carpet, pad and the bottom section of drywall, wash and sanitize hard surfaces, then dry the wall cavities with air movers and LGR dehumidifiers until moisture readings fall back to normal.

Water that keeps returning is a different problem. Restoration repairs what water already damaged, while grading, drainage and waterproofing stop future entry and belong to contractors in those trades. We will say so plainly if that is what your property needs.

Garage storage is a common casualty. Lift boxes, tools and paper goods onto shelves as soon as it is safe, and photograph everything before moving it so the record is ready for a claim.

Sewage Backup Cleanup: When the Drain Backs Up Into the House

If raw sewage is coming up a floor drain or toilet, the job is called sewage backup cleanup, and it is treated as Category 3 water. Under S500 terms that means contaminated, so porous materials it touches, such as carpet, pad and drywall, are removed rather than cleaned. Hard surfaces are cleaned and disinfected.

Keep children and pets away and stay out of the area. If the blockage may be in the city's line rather than your own, the City of Coachella Utilities Department lists a wastewater standby number on coachella.org for sewer overflows. Its office is closed on Fridays, so on a Friday or after hours that standby line is the one to use.

Crews wear protective equipment, contain the area with barriers and run HEPA air scrubbers when odor or airborne debris is a concern. Please do not flush toilets or run water until the blockage is cleared, because that pushes more into the house.

What Water Category Means for Flood Work

Water categories at a glance
CategoryTypical sourceWhat happens to the materials
Category 1A clean supply line, or rain at the moment it entersExtract and dry, and most materials can be saved
Category 2Appliance overflow or water that has picked up contaminantsExtract, remove porous materials, clean and dry
Category 3Sewage, or flood water that has crossed streets, yards or drainsRemove porous materials, disinfect, dry and verify

Floodwater that has crossed a street, yard or drain is generally treated as Category 3, so do not assume a flood is the clean kind.

How Flood Cleanup Proceeds

1

Assess

Moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera show how far water traveled.

2

Extract

Submersible pumps and extraction units remove standing water and mud.

3

Remove and clean

Materials that cannot be saved come out, and surfaces are washed and sanitized.

4

Dry and verify

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run until readings meet the dry standard.

Controlled drying continues under water mitigation, and once readings are verified, water damage repair rebuilds drywall, flooring and paint. Wet materials that sat for days may also call for mold remediation as part of the plan.

Flood Damage Restoration Questions

Do you handle flood damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Coachella?

Yes. The dispatch line is answered 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays, and same-day response is available when crews can reach you. Tell the dispatcher what is wet and whether the source is stopped so the right equipment is sent.

Does homeowners insurance cover flood damage in California?

The California Department of Insurance says homeowners policies typically exclude flood. Flood coverage usually comes from the National Flood Insurance Program or a private flood policy, so check your policy or ask your insurer.

Is flood restoration the same as basement waterproofing?

No. Restoration cleans, removes and dries what water damaged. Waterproofing and foundation inspection stop water entering later, and that belongs to a waterproofing or foundation contractor.

Can I stay in the house during sewage cleanup?

It is best to stay out of the affected area. Keep children and pets away, and ask the crew which rooms are safe to use while containment is in place.

What should I photograph before cleanup starts?

Photograph the water line on walls, each affected room and damaged items before anything is moved. Keep receipts for emergency costs. Moisture readings and photos taken during the job add to that record.
