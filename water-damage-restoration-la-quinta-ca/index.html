La Quinta homes by era, and what each one tends to leak

La Quinta was incorporated in 1982 and grew quickly from the mid-1980s. That means many homes here are now around four decades old, which is an age when original water heaters, supply lines and shutoff valves wear out. If yours has never been replaced, an inspection of the heater and the lines beneath each sink is cheaper than a flooded floor.

Handle these steps first, and skip any that feel unsafe.

Shut the water

Close the valve under the fixture or the main shutoff at the street side of the house. Know both before an emergency.

Protect the panel

Turn off breakers to wet rooms only if the panel is dry and you are on a dry surface.

Raise and remove

Lift rugs, electronics and cabinet contents, and pull furniture off wet floors.

Avoid contamination

Do not wade in water from a sewer, a toilet or storm runoff, and keep children and pets away.
