Terms

Using This Website

This website gives general information about water damage and mold in Coachella, California, and nearby towns. It is not legal, insurance, engineering or medical advice. Every property and every loss is different, so rely on a written scope of work and on your own insurer, building office or physician for decisions that belong to them.

Emergencies

If there is a fire, a gas smell, sparks or any risk to life, call 911 first. Call your utility for electrical or gas problems before you call us. Our line is for restoration, not for emergency rescue.

Scope, Price and Results

The scope of work and how the price is set are explained before work starts, and they depend on the water category, the area and materials affected, the drying time and the repair needed. Nothing on this site promises an arrival time, a price, an insurance outcome or a specific result.

Restoration often involves a safety judgment on site. A crew may decline to enter or work in an area that is unsafe, such as a room with live electrical hazards or an unstable ceiling, until the hazard is resolved.
