Privacy Policy

This page explains what information is involved when you call us or visit this website.

Privacy

Information We Receive

This is a call-only service. The website has no forms and does not ask you to type personal details. When you call, we receive your phone number and whatever you choose to tell us, such as your address, the type of damage and your insurer.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. By continuing a call, you agree to that recording.

Please do not share payment card numbers, government ID numbers or account passwords on a call unless we ask for a specific item and explain why. Nothing about restoring your home requires them in the first minutes.

How We Use It

We use what you tell us to understand the problem, route a crew, explain the scope and price and keep records of the work. We keep call details only as long as they are needed for those purposes and for our legal and business records.

Website Data

The pages load fonts and maps from Google, so your browser contacts Google when you view them, and Google's own privacy policy applies to that traffic. Our host may also keep standard technical logs, such as your IP address and the pages requested, for security and reliability.

We do not run advertising on this website, and the pages do not contain forms, chat boxes or sign-up prompts.

Your Choices

You can ask what we hold about your call, or ask us to correct or delete it where the law allows, by writing to contact@coachellawaterdamagepros.com. We will reply as soon as we reasonably can.

If you are calling on behalf of a landlord, a tenant or an insurer, tell us so at the start of the call. Details about other people are used only to arrange and document the work.

Changes

We may update this policy when our practices change. The version on this page is current as of 2026.
