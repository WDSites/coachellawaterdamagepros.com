If Water Is Still Spreading in Indio

Do these while you wait for the crew, and skip any step that is not safe.

Stop the source

Close the shutoff valve at the fixture or the main valve at the house. If you cannot find it, say so on the call.

Power down wet areas

Switch off breakers to wet rooms only if you can reach the panel while standing on dry ground.

Lift what you can

Move rugs, electronics and paper off the floor and pull furniture away from wet walls.

Keep people and pets out

Water from a toilet, sewer or storm runoff can carry contamination, so keep everyone out of it.
