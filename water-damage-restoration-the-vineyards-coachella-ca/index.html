Water Sources Specific to a Golf Community

Homes beside fairways and common areas often have large irrigation systems. A broken sprinkler line or a valve that sticks open can saturate soil beside a foundation and push water toward a slab edge or a garage door. Look for soggy ground, a lawn patch that stays wet and a water meter that moves when the taps are off.

Shut off the irrigation controller and the yard valve, then photograph the wet area. Water that has reached flooring or drywall needs measured drying, and the sprinkler repair itself belongs to a plumber or irrigation contractor.

Tile Roofs and Rain

After the remnants of Tropical Storm Rachel crossed the valley in October 2026, a local roofer named flat roofs with ponded water, tile roof underlayment, clogged drains and gutters with palm and tamarisk debris, and evaporative coolers dripping onto roofs as common causes of leaks. Those are general valley causes, not findings about this community.

A ceiling stain after rain deserves a look from a roofer as well as a restoration crew. Drying a ceiling without fixing the roof leaves the room open to the next storm.

Recreation Rooms and Shared Spaces

A recreation room, a clubhouse kitchen or a restroom behind a tennis court is closer to a business space than a house, and the owner of the space is often an association. A loss there follows the commercial water damage approach, with a clear chain of who authorizes work.

Safety Steps

  1. Stop the water at the nearest shutoff, whether it is the house main, an appliance valve or the irrigation valve.
  2. Do not touch switches or breakers while standing in water, and stay away from a ceiling that sags.
  3. Photograph the source, the wet surfaces and any visible damage.
  4. Lift belongings off wet floors and keep pets and children away.
  5. Call a plumber for the repair and a restoration company for the water and drying.

A sprinkler line is flooding beside my foundation. Is that water damage?

It can become one if water reaches flooring or walls. Shut the irrigation valve first, photograph the area and call if anything inside is wet. The sprinkler repair is plumbing or irrigation work.

Do I call the association or you first?

Stop the water first. If the loss touches common walls, shared roofs or common areas, notify the association promptly, because its rules may decide who authorizes work.

Will insurance cover rain damage through a tile roof?

That depends on the policy and the cause. Sudden storm damage and a long-neglected leak are often treated differently, so ask your insurer. We make no promise about coverage.
